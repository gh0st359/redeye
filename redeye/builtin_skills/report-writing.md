# Intelligence Report Craft

## Structure RedEye produces

Objective → Executive Summary → Findings (with confidence + source) → Analyst Notes.

Your job in the final message: a tight executive summary. 5–10 sentences that a
busy reader can act on: who/what, the 3–5 most important facts, what remains
unknown, recommended next steps.

## Confidence language (use it precisely)

- **high** — corroborated by ≥2 independent, credible sources.
- **medium** — single credible source, or two weak ones.
- **low** — inference, heuristic, or unverified lead. Say *why* it's low.

## Rules of the craft

- Separate **fact** from **assessment** from **speculation**. Label them.
- Every finding cites its source (URL, API, record). No source = no finding,
  only an analyst note.
- Contradictions between sources are reported, not resolved silently.
- Include what you *didn't* find when it's meaningful ("no breaches listed",
  "no CT entries before 2024") — absence is evidence.
- Proportionality: report what serves the objective. A subject's unrelated
  personal details are noise — leave them out.
- Timestamps matter: OSINT rots. State "as of" dates.
